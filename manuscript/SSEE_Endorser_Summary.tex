\subsection*{Core idea in one sentence}

The core cosmological background — $w_0$, $w_a$, $H_0$, $\Omega_m$, $n_s$ — is derived \emph{algebraically} from the
golden ratio $\varphi=(1+\sqrt{5})/2$ and $\pi$ with \textbf{zero fitted dimensionless parameters at the background level}. Dark-matter sector observables
($\alpha_K$, $m_\varphi$) are formulated as a working EFT proposal subject to full Boltzmann validation, with a handful of phenomenological ans\"atze ($m_\varphi$ OP-9, $\xi$ OP-11) tracked as open problems in \texttt{OPEN\_PROBLEMS.md} (OP-9/11/14; the former MIRA matter mechanism OP-8 is now dissolved under the $\omega_m$-direct reframe).

  \item $r=\varphi^{-10}=0.00813$ — LiteBIRD ($\sim$2032) will confirm or refute the tensor-to-scalar ratio.
  \item A $>3\sigma$ deviation of $w_0$ from $-0.840$ would falsify the corresponding background sector of the framework.
\end{itemize}
