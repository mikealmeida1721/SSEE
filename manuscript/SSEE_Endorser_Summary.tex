\subsection*{Core idea in one sentence}

The core cosmological background — $w_0$, $w_a$, $H_0$, $\Omega_m$, $n_s$ — is derived \emph{algebraically} from the
golden ratio $\varphi=(1+\sqrt{5})/2$ and $\pi$ with \textbf{zero fitted dimensionless parameters at the background level}. Dark-matter sector observables
($\alpha_K$) is formulated as a working EFT proposal subject to full Boltzmann validation. The dark-sector ans\"atze that previously appeared here ($m_\varphi$, $\xi$) were retracted in 2026-08 together with the second matter sector, and their register entries (OP-9 through OP-12) close by dissolution; the MIRA matter mechanism (OP-8) had already dissolved under the $\omega_m$-direct reframe. The remaining open entries are listed in \texttt{OPEN\_PROBLEMS.md}.
