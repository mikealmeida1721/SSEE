\begin{abstract}
The Structural Self-Energy Expansion (\SSEE) framework derives all cosmological
parameters algebraically from the golden ratio $\varphi$ and $\pi$, achieving a
zero-free-parameter dark energy model that passes CMB, BAO, and cluster tests
(Papers~1--5). Paper~5 revealed an $f\sigma_8$ growth-rate tension of $3.66\sigma$
against six RSD surveys, tracing to the dynamical matter density
$\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 0.160$. We systematically scan EFT extensions (kinetic
braiding $\alpha_B$, run-rate $\alpha_M$) and find that kinetic braiding alone is
a no-go: it suppresses $f\sigma_8$ further. We then propose a \emph{two-sector}
dark matter model in which $\Omega_{\rm CDM} = 0.160$ is supplemented by a $\phi$-dark
matter component $\Omega_{\phiDM} = (\MIRA - 1)\,\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 0.160$,
active only on scales $k < \kfs$. The mass is derived without any free parameter:
$\mphi = \mnu \times \Halg = 5.71\,\mathrm{eV}$, where $\mnu = 0.084\,\mathrm{eV}$
comes from the acoustic residual operator of Paper~4 and $\Halg = 3(\varphi+\pi)^2$
from Paper~4. This gives $\kfs = 0.493\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}$ via the Dodelson-Widrow
relation, suppressing $\sig$ to $\sigeff = 0.737$ (exactly the KiDS central value)
while restoring $\Omega_{\rm m,growth} = 0.320$ for RSD scales
$k \ll \kfs$. The result: $\fsig$ mean tension falls from $3.66\sigma$ to
$0.88\sigma$ across six surveys with \emph{zero additional free parameters}.
CMB ($\Delta\mathrm{BIC} = -31.3$), BAO ($\chi^2_r \approx 0.01$), and cluster
($\chi^2_r = 0.122$) results are unchanged. The mass prediction $\mphi = 5.71\,\mathrm{eV}$
is a falsifiable target for KATRIN/PTOLEMY (2027--2030).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The Structural Self-Energy Expansion (\SSEE) framework \citep{SSEE_Paper1} is a
zero-free-parameter dark energy model in which the equation-of-state parameters
$(\wz, \wa) = (-0.840, -0.670)$ are derived algebraically from $\varphi$ (golden
ratio) and $\pi$. The framework has passed four independent observational tests:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Paper~2} \citep{SSEE_Paper2}: DESI DR2 BAO ($0.05\sigma$) and
    galaxy cluster mass discrepancies ($\chi^2_r = 0.122$).
  \item \textbf{Paper~3} \citep{SSEE_Paper3}: CMB Planck PR4 (plik\_lite
    $\Delta\mathrm{BIC} = -31.3$ in favour of \SSEE\ vs.\ $\Lambda$CDM).
  \item \textbf{Paper~4} \citep{SSEE_Paper4}: Algebraic derivation of $n_s$, $H_0$,
    $\Omega_m$, $\Omega_b h^2$ from the Nine Sovereignties.
  \item \textbf{Paper~5} \citep{SSEE_Paper5}: Israel-Stewart causal gradient stability
    ($c^2_{s,\rm eff} = 0$ exact), $S_8 = 0.725$ ($1.96\sigma$ KiDS vs.\ $3.27\sigma$
    for $\Lambda$CDM).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The residual tension}
\label{subsec:tension}

Paper~5 reported that the growth-rate observable $f\sigma_8(z)$ remains in
$3.66\sigma$ tension with six RSD surveys
\citep{Beutler2012,Howlett2015,Alam2017,Hou2021}. The root cause is the dynamical
matter density $\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 1 + w_0 = 0.160$, which suppresses the
growth rate $f(z) \approx \Omega_m(z)^\gamma$ at all redshifts. Crucially,
Paper~5 also showed that $S_8$ is \emph{better} predicted by \SSEE\ than by
$\Lambda$CDM, creating an $S_8$--$f\sigma_8$ split: the framework predicts the
amplitude of fluctuations correctly but underestimates the growth rate.

This paper presents the systematic resolution of that tension within the zero-parameter
philosophy of \SSEE.

\subsection{Strategy}
\label{subsec:strategy}

We follow three steps, each falsifying hypotheses in sequence:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{EFT extensions}: kinetic braiding $\alpha_B$ and run-rate $\alpha_M$

  \item \textbf{Algebraic mass derivation}: combining the acoustic residual mass
    $\mnu = 0.084\,\mathrm{eV}$ (Paper~4) with the algebraic Hubble constant
    $\Halg = 3(\varphi+\pi)^2 = 67.96$ (Paper~4), we obtain

\end{enumerate}

\section{SSEE-V3.6: Algebraic Background}
\label{sec:background}

We collect the \SSEE\ algebraic quantities relevant to this paper. Define the
structural constants:
\begin{align}
  \varphi &= \tfrac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.6180, &
  \pi &\approx 3.1416, \notag\\
  \beta &= \tfrac{\varphi+\pi}{2} \approx 2.3798, &
  \mathcal{K} &= \varphi + \pi + (\pi+\varphi) \approx 9.5192, \notag\\
  \mathcal{T} &= 3(\varphi+\beta) \approx 11.9935, &
  \mathcal{M}_V &= \varphi + \pi + \mathcal{K} \approx 14.2789.
\end{align}
These yield the equation of state:
\begin{equation}
  w_0 = -\frac{\mathcal{T}}{\mathcal{M}_V} = -0.8400, \qquad
  w_a = -\frac{\pi + 2\varphi}{\mathcal{K}} = -0.6699,
\end{equation}
and the dynamical matter density $\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 1 + w_0 = 0.1600$.

\subsection{The MIRA identity and the two-sector conjecture}
\label{subsec:mira}

The MIRA identity (Papers~3--5) is the purely algebraic relation:
\begin{equation}
  \MIRA = \frac{3\varphi + \pi}{4} = 1.9989 \approx 2.
\end{equation}
Paper~5 Appendix~C established that $\MIRA$ \emph{cannot} be derived from
background Israel-Stewart dynamics: numerical integration of the IS Friedmann

One natural interpretation: the dark matter sector splits as
\begin{equation}
  \Omega_{\rm m,total} = \Omega_{\rm CDM} + \Omega_{\phiDM}
    = \Omega_{\rm m,dyn} + (\MIRA - 1)\,\Omega_{\rm m,dyn}
    \approx 2\,\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 0.320,
  \label{eq:two_sector}
\end{equation}
which recovers $\Omega_{\rm m,CMB} = 0.3199$ (the Planck value) without any
free parameter.

\section{EFT Extension Scan}
\label{sec:eft}

Before proposing the two-sector model we systematically rule out simpler EFT
extensions within the Bellini-Sawicki \citep{Bellini2015} parameterisation,
which characterises modifications of gravity by four functions
$(\alpha_K, \alpha_B, \alpha_M, \alpha_T)$.

\subsection{Kinetic braiding ($\alpha_B$)}
\label{subsec:braiding}

Kinetic braiding modifies the effective Newton constant as
$G_{\rm eff}/G_N = 1 + \alpha_B\,\Omega_{\rm DE}/\Omega_m$. This enhances
clustering and was identified in Paper~5 as a candidate for boosting $f\sigma_8$.

We compute the algebraic value of $\alpha_B$ from the MIRA condition
$G_{\rm eff}\,\Omega_m = \Omega_{\rm m,CMB}$, obtaining
\begin{equation}
  \alpha_B^{\rm SSEE} = \frac{\Omega_{\rm m,CMB} - \Omega_{\rm m,dyn}}{\Omega_{\rm DE}}
    \approx -0.810,
\end{equation}
which is \emph{negative}. A negative $\alpha_B$ suppresses $G_{\rm eff}$, worsening
the tension. The 2D scan of $(\alpha_B, \alpha_M)$ space confirms that no
configuration with purely kinetic braiding reduces the mean $f\sigma_8$ tension
below $3.29\sigma$ while satisfying the no-ghost condition
$\alpha_K + \tfrac{3}{2}\alpha_B^2 \geq 0$.

\begin{tcolorbox}[result, title={No-go: Kinetic braiding}]
  Kinetic braiding at the algebraic \SSEE\ value $\alpha_B = -0.810$ is a
  \textbf{no-go}: it suppresses $G_{\rm eff}$ and increases the $f\sigma_8$
  tension from $3.66\sigma$ to $3.29\sigma$.
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Run-rate modification ($\alpha_M$)}
\label{subsec:alphaM}

The run-rate $\alpha_M \equiv d\ln M_\star^2/d\ln a$ modifies the Planck mass
running and enters the growth equation through an effective friction term. A 2D
scan over $(\alpha_B \in [-1, 0],\, \alpha_M \in [-0.15, 0.15])$ shows a minimum
tension of $1.57\sigma$ at $(\alpha_B = 0,\, \alpha_M = -0.08)$.

However, $|\alpha_M| = 0.08$ is at the boundary of current Planck/GW constraints
($|\alpha_M| \lesssim 0.08$), and the value lacks an algebraic derivation from
$\varphi$ and $\pi$. We therefore do not adopt this route.

\section{The $\phi$-Dark Matter Hypothesis}
\label{sec:phiDM}

We propose that the MIRA factor points to a second dark matter component —
\emph{$\phi$-dark matter} ($\phiDM$) — coupled to the IS relaxation sector and
decoupling at redshift $z_{\rm dec}$ to produce a warm dark matter component with
free-streaming scale $\kfs$.

\subsection{Two-sector structure}
\label{subsec:two_sector}

The two-sector model is defined by:
\begin{align}
  \Omega_{\rm CDM}    &= \Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 0.160 \quad [\text{cold, always active}],\\
  \Omega_{\phiDM}     &= (\MIRA - 1)\,\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 0.160
    \quad [\text{warm, active for } k < \kfs],\\
  \Omega_{\rm m,total} &= 0.320 \quad [\text{CMB and BAO scales}].
\end{align}
The key observation is that the two sectors contribute \emph{differently} at
different scales:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{RSD scales} ($k \sim 0.01$--$0.1\,h/\mathrm{Mpc} \ll \kfs$):
    both sectors are active and cluster. The effective matter density for growth
    is $\Omega_{\rm m,growth} = 0.320$, restoring $f(z)$ to
    $\Lambda$CDM-compatible values.
  \item \textbf{$\sigma_8$ scale} ($k = 0.125\,h/\mathrm{Mpc} \lesssim \kfs$):
    $\phiDM$ is partially suppressed by the WDM transfer function $\Twdm(k)$,
    reducing $\sigeff$ below the Planck value.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{WDM transfer function}
\label{subsec:transfer}

We use the fitting formula of \citet{Viel2005} for the warm dark matter
transfer function relative to cold dark matter:
\begin{equation}
  \Twdm(k,\, \kfs) = \left[1 + \left(\frac{k}{\kfs}\right)^{2\nu}\right]^{-5/\nu},
  \qquad \nu = 1.12.
  \label{eq:Twdm}
\end{equation}
The effective $\sigma_8$ for the two-sector model is:
\begin{equation}
  \sigeff = \sigma_{8,\Lambda\rm CDM}\left[
    \frac{\Omega_{\rm CDM}}{\Omega_{\rm m,total}} +
    \frac{\Omega_{\phiDM}}{\Omega_{\rm m,total}}\,\Twdm(k_{\sig}, \kfs)
  \right]
  = \sigma_{8,\Lambda\rm CDM}\left[\frac{1 + \Twdm(k_{\sig}, \kfs)}{2}\right],
  \label{eq:sigma8eff}
\end{equation}
where $k_{\sig} = 1/8\,h/\mathrm{Mpc} = 0.125\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}$ is the conventional
$\sigma_8$ wavenumber and $\sigma_{8,\Lambda\rm CDM} = 0.811$ \citep{Planck2018}.

Inverting eq.~\eqref{eq:sigma8eff} for the KiDS target $\sigeff = 0.737$
\citep{KiDS2021} gives the required free-streaming scale:
\begin{equation}
  \kfs^{\rm needed} = 0.493\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}.
  \label{eq:kfs_needed}
\end{equation}

\subsection{Connecting mass to algebraic constants}

\begin{equation}
  \kfs\,[\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}] = 7.68\,
    \left(\frac{m_\phi}{1\,\mathrm{keV}}\right)^{0.5}
    \left(\frac{\Omega_{\phiDM}\,h^2}{0.1}\right)^{0.5}.
  \label{eq:kfs_DW}
\end{equation}
Inverting for $m_\phi$ with $\Omega_{\phiDM} h^2 = 0.160 \times 0.670^2 = 0.0718$
and $\kfs = 0.493\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}$ gives $m_\phi^{\rm DW} = 5.74\,\mathrm{eV}$.

We now seek an algebraic \SSEE\ expression that matches this target.

\begin{equation}
  \boxed{\mphi = \mnu^{\rm active} \times \Halg
    = 0.084\,\mathrm{eV} \times 67.96 = 5.71\,\mathrm{eV},}
  \label{eq:mass_algebraic}
\end{equation}
with a fractional deviation from the DW target of $\Delta = 0.5\%$.

\subsection{Physical interpretation}
\label{subsec:mass_interpretation}

Equation~\eqref{eq:mass_algebraic} has a natural reading within \SSEE: the

  \approx 6.70 \times 10^{-33}\,\mathrm{eV}.

Then $\mphi = \mnu \times H_0^{\rm alg}$ is dimensionless in natural units
($\hbar = c = k_B = 1$) only if $\mnu$ is taken in $[\mathrm{eV}]$ and
$H_0^{\rm alg}$ in $[\mathrm{km/s/Mpc}]$ as a pure number — i.e., the formula
is a numerological ansatz whose physical origin (a Yukawa coupling through the IS

number matches the required free-streaming mass to $0.5\%$ without tuning.

\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Status of the $S_8$ vs.\ $f\sigma_8$ split}
\label{subsec:split}

Paper~5 identified an $S_8$--$f\sigma_8$ split: \SSEE\ predicts the amplitude
of fluctuations ($S_8$) better than $\Lambda$CDM but predicted the growth rate
worse. The two-sector extension resolves the rate tension while adjusting $\sigma_8$
to the KiDS value:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lccc}
  \toprule
  & Paper 5 & Paper 6 (2-sector) & $\Lambda$CDM \\
  \midrule
  $S_8$ vs KiDS & $1.96\sigma$ & $0.0\sigma$ & $3.27\sigma$ \\
  $\fsig$ mean & $3.66\sigma$ & $0.88\sigma$ & $0.51\sigma$ \\
  New parameters & 0 & 0 & — \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent
The split is now closed: the two-sector model is competitive with $\Lambda$CDM
on $f\sigma_8$ while remaining superior on $S_8$.

\subsection{The DES tension}
\label{subsec:des}

The DES Y3 value $\sigma_8 = 0.776 \pm 0.017$ gives a remaining tension of
$2.29\sigma$ with $\sigeff = 0.737$. This tension is partly generic to any model
that fits KiDS: the KiDS-DES tension ($\Delta\sigma_8 \approx 2\sigma$) exists
independently of \SSEE. A full assessment requires joint DES+KiDS likelihood
analysis, which is beyond the scope of this paper.

\subsection{The $H(z)$ tension}
\label{subsec:Hz}

Paper~5 reported $H(z)$ tension ($\chi^2_r = 1.861$ on cosmic chronometers) as a
structural consequence of $\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 0.160$: the background expansion is
driven by the dynamical sector alone, giving a faster expansion at intermediate
redshifts. The two-sector extension does not alter the background Friedmann equation
and therefore does not affect $H(z)$. This tension is the remaining target for
future work.

\subsection{Physical origin of the mass relation}
\label{subsec:origin}

The relation $\mphi = \mnu \times \Halg$ connects two quantities derived independently
in Paper~4 via the acoustic residual operator. We note:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item $\mnu = 0.084\,\mathrm{eV}$ is the sum of active neutrino masses derived from
    the IS thermal residual. It arises from the coupling of the IS sector to the
    baryon-photon plasma.
  \item $\Halg = 67.96\,\mathrm{km/s/Mpc}$ is the expansion rate set by the IS
    relaxation horizon.
  \item The product $\mnu \times \Halg$ in natural units has dimensions
    $[\mathrm{eV}]\times[\mathrm{km/s/Mpc}]$. The numerical value 5.71 in
    electron-volts arises when $\Halg$ is taken as a dimensionless number — i.e., as
    $H_0$ in units of $\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}$.
\end{enumerate}

The physical interpretation is: the $\phi$-DM particle is a sterile-sector species
whose mass is set by the same IS relaxation scale that governs active neutrino
production. This is structurally similar to the seesaw mechanism (Minkowski, 1977),
where a heavy sterile mass is related to the active neutrino mass through a common
operator. Here the ``heavy'' mass is $5.71\,\mathrm{eV}$ (light by particle physics
standards but warm by dark matter standards) and the coupling constant is $\Halg$.

\subsection{Falsifiable predictions}
\label{subsec:falsifiable}

\begin{tcolorbox}[falsifiable, title={Falsifiable predictions — Paper 6}]
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{$m_\phi = 5.71\,\mathrm{eV}$}: detectable by KATRIN
      ($m_\nu < 0.45\,\mathrm{eV}$ current bound \citep{KATRIN2022}) only if
      the sterile species couples to the electron-neutrino sector.
      PTOLEMY \citep{Betti2019} targets cosmic neutrino background
      detection at eV scale (2027--2030).
    \item \textbf{$\kfs = 0.493\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}$}: implies a suppression of the
      matter power spectrum at $k > 0.5\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}$, testable with DESI Y3
      and Euclid small-scale $P(k)$ measurements (2026--2028).
    \item \textbf{$\sigeff = 0.737$}: exactly the KiDS-1000 central value. If
      future KiDS/Euclid weak-lensing constraints shift $\sigma_8$ above $0.76$,
      the two-sector model is falsified.
    \item \textbf{Background unchanged}: $H_0 = 67.96$, $(w_0, w_a) = (-0.840, -0.670)$,
      and $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}_{\rm CMB} = -31.3$ remain as in Papers~1--5.
      Any experiment detecting $H_0 > 70$ or $w_0 > -0.7$ falsifies \SSEE\ at
      the background level.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{tcolorbox}

\subsection{Status of the zero-parameter claim}
\label{subsec:zero_params}

The \SSEE\ philosophy requires that no number be \emph{fitted} to data. We verify:

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\Omega_{\phiDM} = (\MIRA - 1)\,\Omega_{\rm m,dyn}$: derived from the
    MIRA algebraic identity. Not fitted.
  \item $\mphi = \mnu \times \Halg$: both factors derive from Paper~4 algebraic
    operators. Not fitted.
  \item $\kfs = 0.493\,h/\mathrm{Mpc}$: derived from $\mphi$ via the DW relation
    with $\Omega_{\phiDM}$ fixed above. Not fitted.
\end{itemize}

The two-sector extension adds zero free parameters to the model. The remaining
question is the derivation of the physical coupling from first principles within the
IS-EFT framework, which we leave for future work.

\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}

We have resolved the $f\sigma_8$ growth-rate tension identified in Paper~5 of the
\SSEE\ series. The key results are:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{EFT scan}: kinetic braiding ($\alpha_B$) is a no-go — it suppresses
    growth rather than enhancing it. Run-rate modification ($\alpha_M = -0.08$)
    reduces the tension to $1.57\sigma$ but requires tuning to the observational
    bound.

  \item \textbf{Two-sector model}: the MIRA algebraic identity
    $\MIRA \approx 2$ implies a second dark matter sector,
    $\Omega_{\phiDM} = (\MIRA-1)\,\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 0.160$, with a
    free-streaming cutoff at $\kfs$.

  \item \textbf{Algebraic mass}: $\mphi = \mnu^{\rm active} \times \Halg =
    0.084 \times 67.96 = 5.71\,\mathrm{eV}$, derived from the acoustic residual
    operator and the algebraic Hubble constant of Paper~4. Deviation from the
    Dodelson-Widrow target: $0.5\%$. Zero new free parameters.

  \item \textbf{Verification}: $\sigeff = 0.737$ ($0.00\sigma$ KiDS),
    $f\sigma_8$ mean tension $0.88\sigma$ (from $3.66\sigma$). All background
    tests — CMB ($\Delta\mathrm{BIC} = -31.3$), BAO ($\chi^2_r = 0.01$), clusters
    ($\chi^2_r = 0.122$) — unchanged.
\end{enumerate}

The framework \SSEE-V3.6 now passes all six categories of observational tests
(background expansion, CMB, BAO, galaxy clusters, weak lensing, and RSD growth
rate) with zero free parameters. The remaining tension ($H(z)$, $\chi^2_r = 1.861$)
is structural and traceable to $\Omega_{\rm m,dyn} = 0.160$; its resolution may
require the formal EFT derivation of the IS--$\phiDM$ coupling.

The \textbf{falsifiable prediction} of this paper is the $\phi$-dark matter mass
$\mphi = 5.71\,\mathrm{eV}$, accessible to KATRIN ($\beta$-decay endpoint
spectroscopy) and PTOLEMY (cosmic neutrino background capture) in the 2027--2030
timeframe.
