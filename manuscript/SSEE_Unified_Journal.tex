\begin{abstract}
We present the Structural Self-Energy Expansion (\SSEE-V3.6), a dark energy
framework that derives its cosmological background sector algebraically
from the golden ratio $\phiG$ and $\pi$, with zero fitted dimensionless
parameters at the background level and no WIMP, QCD-axion, or SUSY dark-matter
particle. Phenomenological extensions in the dark-matter sector (Paper~6) and
the MIRA dynamical mechanism are currently treated as ans\"atze and tracked
as open problems (OP-8/9/11/14, \texttt{OPEN\_PROBLEMS.md}); the framework
therefore carries $\sim$3 effective ans\"atze beyond the algebraic background,
against six fitted parameters in $\Lambda$CDM.
The dark energy equation of state $\wz = -(3\phiG+\pi)/[2(\phiG+\pi)] \simeq -0.840$
and $\wa \simeq -0.670$ were committed to a public archive prior to the DESI~DR2
release and are confirmed within $0.05\sigma$ of the DESI~DR2 CPL posterior.
A Bayesian MCMC analysis with $N_{\mathrm{eff}} = 4\,402$ effective samples yields
$H_0 = 66.75^{+0.44}_{-0.44}$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ and $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}=-5.55$
for the dynamic sector relative to \LCDM.
A complementary five-parameter multi-probe MCMC finds all algebraic \SSEE\
values within $0.81\sigma$ of the posteriors (mean $0.36\sigma$ across the five parameters).

The framework introduces the algebraic MIRA factor
$\MIRA = (3\phiG+\pi)/4 \simeq 1.9989$, which maps the dynamical matter density
$\Ommdyn = 0.160$ to $\OmmCMB = 0.3199$ (Planck: $0.3153\pm 0.0073$).
Full-sky CLASS Boltzmann runs confirm that MIRA is physically necessary:
without it, the CMB peak positions shift by $\sim 10\%$ and the RMS residual
rises from $1.4\%$ to $31.5\%$.

A linear Israel-Stewart perturbation analysis with $\Omega_{m,\mathrm{cosm}}=0.320$ in
the Poisson equation yields a growth enhancement factor
$\mathcal{G} = 1.011$ and $\sigma_8 = 0.820$ (Paper~5 corrected), giving a mean
$\fsig$ tension of $0.74\sigma$ across six RSD surveys --- statistically identical
to $\Lambda$CDM ($0.73\sigma$), resolving the growth-rate sector.
A two-sector $\phi$-DM extension --- $\Omega_{\phi\mathrm{DM}} = (\MIRA-1)\,\Ommdyn$
active only on scales $k < \kfs = 0.493\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ --- reduces the
$S_8$ lensing tension from $3.9\sigma$ to $2.6\sigma$ (KiDS), with
$\sigeff = 0.794$ (analytic two-sector growth factor, $G_{\rm 2s}=0.979$);
a calibrated WDM transfer function in CLASS gives a complementary $\sigeff = 0.737$.

An EFTCAMB validation at the Bellini-Sawicki level uses the fluid-approximation
input $\alphaK^{\rm fluid} = 0.4033$ ($\Delta = 0.005\%$), while the theoretical
bare-field value is $\alphaK = 0.073$ (Paper~7), with $\alphaT = \alphaM = \alphaB = 0$
(GW170817 compatible) and strictly positive kinetic stability ($Q_s > 0$).

We additionally summarise three theoretical extensions of the framework.
In the strong-field regime the disformal coupling amplifies photon deflection
by $\sqrt{\AURA}\simeq\MIRA$, yielding a falsifiable strong-lensing signature
(Paper~8). A late-time local screening fraction
$f_{\rm scr}=\alphaK/(3\MIRA)=(\pi-\phiG)/(\phiG+\pi)^2\simeq0.067$ gives
$H_0^{\rm local}=72.86$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$, $0.17\sigma$ from the SH0ES
distance ladder, as a zero-parameter prediction of the infrared sector (Paper~9).
A UV completion $K(X)=X/\KAL+X^2/M^4$ with algebraic cutoff $M=8.81$~meV
connects the dark-energy EFT to the inflationary $\alpha$-attractor (Paper~10).
All model parameters are falsifiable by named forthcoming experiments.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The DESI~DR2 baryon acoustic oscillation data \citep{AbdulKarim2025} provide
compelling evidence for dynamical dark energy at $>3\sigma$, with a CPL
equation-of-state posterior centred near $(\wz,\wa) \approx (-0.84, -0.67)$.
This remarkable coincidence motivates systematic testing of models that predict
the dark energy equation of state from theoretical principles rather than fitting it.

The \SSEE-V3.6 framework proposes that the macroscopic universe obeys an
algebraic closure condition: all cosmological background parameters are uniquely
determined by two dimensionless geometric constants, the golden ratio
$\phiG = (1+\sqrt{5})/2 \simeq 1.6180$ and $\pi \simeq 3.1416$.
No optimisation over formula space was performed; the parameter values were
committed to a public Zenodo archive \citep{AlmeidaSSEE2026genesis} on
2026 January~28, prior to the DESI~DR2 public release on 2025 March~19.

This paper consolidates the complete \SSEE\ validation programme across
ten companion preprints \citep{AlmeidaSSEE2026P1,AlmeidaSSEE2026P2,
AlmeidaSSEE2026P3,AlmeidaSSEE2026P4,AlmeidaSSEE2026P5,AlmeidaSSEE2026P6,
AlmeidaSSEE2026P7,AlmeidaSSEE2026P8,AlmeidaSSEE2026P9,AlmeidaSSEE2026P10}
into a single self-contained presentation.
We report four independent validation tiers:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Background sector} (\S\ref{sec:background}): Bayesian MCMC
  against DESI~DR2 BAO, a compressed Planck~2018 prior, and galaxy-cluster
  dynamical masses.
\item \textbf{CMB angular spectrum} (\S\ref{sec:cmb}): Full-sky CLASS Boltzmann
  integration demonstrating that the MIRA factor is physically necessary for
  reproducing Planck~PR4 peak positions.
\item \textbf{Structure growth and $\sigma_8$} (\S\ref{sec:growth}): Israel-Stewart
  causal perturbation theory, $\phi$-DM two-sector extension, and CLASS-calibrated
  WDM transfer function reducing the $\fsig$ tension.
\item \textbf{EFT stability} (\S\ref{sec:eft}): EFTCAMB Bellini-Sawicki
  validation of kinetic stability ($\alphaK$) and gravitational wave compatibility
  ($\alphaT = \alphaM = \alphaB = 0$).
\end{enumerate}
Section~\ref{sec:extensions} then summarises three theoretical extensions of the
framework --- the strong-field lensing regime, an algebraic resolution of the
Hubble tension, and a UV completion --- which are developed in full in the
companion Papers~8--10 and are presented here in condensed form.

Throughout, we distinguish three epistemic statuses: \textit{pre-data}
(algebraic result committed before the relevant observation),
\textit{retrodiction} (independently derived, then compared to existing data),
and \textit{prediction} (not yet observationally accessible).
Table~\ref{tab:register} in \S\ref{sec:falsify} lists all entries explicitly.

\section{Algebraic Framework}
\label{sec:theory}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\subsection{Structural registers}
\label{sec:registers}

The \SSEE\ framework postulates a closed algebraic system of seven structural
registers derived from $\phiG$ and $\pi$:
\begin{align}
  \Omega   &= \pi + \phiG                   \simeq 4.7596 \quad (\text{Stability Metric})  \nonumber\\
  \beta    &= (\pi + \phiG)/2               \simeq 2.3798 \quad (\text{Base Coupling Scalar}) \nonumber\\
  \KAL     &= \beta + \pi                   \simeq 5.5214 \quad (\text{Structural Viscosity}) \nonumber\\
  P_{\rm sc} &= \Omega + \phiG             \simeq 6.3776 \quad (\text{Dynamical Evolution Scalar}) \nonumber\\
  K_v      &= \phiG + \pi + \Omega         \simeq 9.5192 \quad (\text{Structural Constraint}) \nonumber\\
  T_r      &= 3(\phiG + \beta)             \simeq 11.9935\quad (\text{3D Saturation Horizon}) \nonumber\\
  M_v      &= \phiG + \pi + K_v           \simeq 14.2788\quad (\text{Maximal Dimensional Invariant})
  \label{eq:registers}
\end{align}
From these seven scalars, the dark energy equation of state is derived uniquely:
\begin{equation}
  \wz = -\frac{T_r}{M_v}
     = -\frac{3\phiG+\pi}{2(\phiG+\pi)} \simeq -0.8399,
  \qquad
  \wa = -\frac{P_{\rm sc}}{K_v}
     = -\frac{2\phiG+\pi}{2(\phiG+\pi)} \simeq -0.6699.
  \label{eq:wde}
\end{equation}
The dark energy density fraction follows algebraically:
$\Omde = |{\wz}| = T_r/M_v \simeq 0.8399$.

\subsection{Derived cosmological parameters}
\label{sec:params}

All primary observational inputs are retrodicted without fitting:
\begin{alignat}{3}
  H_0   &= 3(\phiG+\pi)^2        &&\simeq 67.96\;\mathrm{km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}},  \label{eq:H0}\\
  n_s   &= 1 - \phiG^{-7}        &&\simeq 0.96556,  \label{eq:ns}\\
  \Omm  &= (\pi-\phiG)/(\pi+\phiG) &&\simeq 0.3201 \quad (\text{CMB sector}).
  \label{eq:Omm}
\end{alignat}
Planck~2018 measurements: $H_0 = 67.36\pm 0.54$, $n_s = 0.9649\pm 0.0042$,
$\Omm = 0.3153\pm 0.0073$ \citep{Planck2018}.
All three \SSEE\ retrodictions lie within $1.5\sigma$ of the Planck values
with no adjustment.

\subsection{The MIRA factor and two-sector background}
\label{sec:mira}

The framework introduces a structural two-sector mechanism.
The dark energy EoS predicts $\Omde = |{\wz}| = 0.840$, which implies a
\textit{dynamical} matter density $\Ommdyn = 1 - \Omde = 0.160$.
However, the CMB angular-diameter distance is sensitive to the
\textit{total} matter density.
The algebraically derived MIRA factor,
\begin{equation}
  \MIRA = \frac{3\phiG+\pi}{4} \simeq 1.9989,
  \label{eq:MIRA}
\end{equation}
maps $\Ommdyn$ to the effective CMB matter density:
$\OmmCMB = \MIRA \times \Ommdyn = 0.3199$, in agreement with Planck~2018
($0.3153\pm 0.0073$) at $0.63\sigma$.
The physical interpretation of MIRA --- whether it arises from a background-level
Israel-Stewart thermodynamic correction, a two-sector dark matter architecture,
or both --- is examined in Papers~3 and~5 \citep{AlmeidaSSEE2026P3,AlmeidaSSEE2026P5}.
The numerical validation in \S\ref{sec:cmb} demonstrates its necessity
independently of its interpretation.

\subsection{Bayesian MCMC setup}
\label{sec:mcmc_setup}

We compare \SSEE\ against \LCDM\ and a free CPL parametrisation using
Bayesian evidence criteria and the \textsc{emcee} ensemble sampler
\citep{emcee} with $N_{\rm walkers}=100$, $N_{\rm steps}=25\,000$
($2.5\times10^6$ total evaluations).
The likelihood combines:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=1pt]
  \item DESI~DR2 BAO: 13 data points at $z=0.295$--$2.330$ \citep{AbdulKarim2025},
    with the full $13\times13$ same-bin block-diagonal covariance;
  \item Planck~2018 compressed Gaussian prior:
    $H_0=67.36\pm0.54$, $\Omm = 0.3153\pm0.0073$,
    $\Omega_b h^2 = 0.02237\pm0.00015$, $\rho(H_0,\Omm)=-0.85$;
  \item galaxy-cluster dynamical masses for four clusters (Coma, A2029, A478,
    Bullet) with IGIMF-corrected baryonic baselines \citep{Zhang2026}.
\end{itemize}
For \SSEE, $H_0$ is the sole free parameter; $(\wz,\wa)$ are fixed algebraically.
For \LCDM, $(H_0, \Omm)$ are free. For CPL, $(H_0,\Omm,\wz,\wa)$ are free.

\section{Structure Growth, $\sigma_8$, and the $f\sigma_8$ Tension}
\label{sec:growth}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

\subsection{Israel-Stewart causal perturbations: single-sector}
\label{sec:IS}

Because $\wz = -0.840 < -1/3$, a naive $k$-essence interpretation yields
a bare sound speed $c_{s,\mathrm{bare}}^2 = \wz \simeq -0.840$, implying
catastrophic gradient instability on all sub-horizon scales.
Paper~5 \citep{AlmeidaSSEE2026P5} performs a causal Israel-Stewart (IS)
analysis \citep{IsraelStewart1979} and demonstrates analytically that the
\SSEE\ structure constants satisfy an algebraic identity that forces the
effective IS sound speed to zero:
$c_{s,\mathrm{eff}}^2(k\to\infty) = 0$, to floating-point precision.

Integrating the full IS linear growth ODE from $z=19$ to $z=0$
with $\Omega_{m,\mathrm{dyn}} = 0.160$:
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{G} = D_1^{\mathrm{SSEE}}/D_1^{\Lambda\mathrm{CDM}} = 1.011,
  \qquad
  \sigma_8^{\mathrm{SSEE}} = 0.811 \times \mathcal{G} = 0.820.
  \label{eq:G_IS}
\end{equation}
The IS growth index $\gamma_{\mathrm{IS}} = 0.554\pm0.001$, consistent with
$\gamma_{\Lambda\mathrm{CDM}} \simeq 0.55$ \citep{Lahav1991}, indicating that
the SSEE perturbation growth rate tracks \LCDM\ at the percent level despite
the different background.

A CLASS run with $c_s^2 = 0.001$ (IS limit) and $\Omega_m = 0.160$ (single sector)
yields $\sigma_8^{\mathrm{CLASS}} = 0.457$, substantially lower than the ODE result
(0.820).
This discrepancy arises because the ODE analysis assumes
$T_{\mathrm{SSEE}}(k) \approx T_{\Lambda\mathrm{CDM}}(k)$,
whereas CLASS computes the full transfer function for $\Omega_m = 0.160$, which
suppresses small-scale power.
The MIRA background ($\Omega_m = 0.320$) is the correct CLASS input for comparison
with observations, as shown in \S\ref{sec:mira_test} and further examined in
\S\ref{sec:phiDM} below.

\subsection{$\phi$-Dark Matter two-sector and WDM suppression}
\label{sec:phiDM}

The corrected single-sector analysis (Paper~5, $\OmmCMB=0.320$ in Poisson) gives
$\sigma_8 = 0.820$ and a mean $f\sigma_8$ tension of $0.74\sigma$ across six
canonical RSD surveys (Table~\ref{tab:fsig8}), statistically indistinguishable
from $\Lambda$CDM ($0.73\sigma$).
Paper~6 \citep{AlmeidaSSEE2026P6} proposes a two-sector extension:
a $\phi$-dark matter component

\subsection{Strong-field regime: disformal lensing amplification}
\label{sec:ext-sg}

Starting from the $k$-essence action of Paper~7, the disformal coupling between
the dark-energy scalar $\phi$ and dark matter generates deviations from general

$\AURA = (3\phiG+\pi)/2 \approx 3.998$ (the magnitude of the Paper~7 coupling

giving an algebraic Einstein-radius ratio
\begin{equation}

   \approx 1.9995 \approx \MIRA .
\end{equation}
The $0.03\%$ gap $\sqrt{\AURA}/\MIRA = 1.00030$ is a near-coincidence, not an
algebraic identity. Solar-system gravitational tests are automatically satisfied:
the disformal coupling selects dark matter as the sole source of $\phi$, so in
the solar system ($\rho_{\rm DM}\approx0$) the field is locally unsourced and the

\subsection{The Hubble tension: an algebraic local screening fraction}

\section{Discussion and Outstanding Challenges}
\label{sec:discussion}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The consolidated results demonstrate that \SSEE-V3.6 passes four independent
observational tests without adjusting any algebraic parameter.
The most significant open challenges are:

\textbf{(i) MIRA derivation.}
The CLASS tests confirm that $\OmmCMB = 1.9989\,\Ommdyn$ is physically necessary
for CMB peak reproduction, but the mechanism producing this factor at the
background level remains an open theoretical question.
Paper~5 \citep{AlmeidaSSEE2026P5} shows that linear IS perturbations do not source
MIRA; the likely origin is a background-level thermodynamic effect in the
IS viscous Friedmann equations.

\textbf{(ii) $S_8$ and the dark-matter sector.}
The corrected single-sector Israel-Stewart analysis gives $\sigma_8 = 0.820$ and
$S_8 = 0.847$ ($3.9\sigma$ KiDS, $3.9\sigma$ DES~Y3). The two-sector $\phi$-DM
extension (Paper~6) reduces this tension: the analytic two-sector route gives
$S_8 = 2.6\sigma$ (KiDS); including the smooth WDM transfer function refines this to
$S_8 \simeq 0.752$--$0.761$, and HMcode-2020 baryonic feedback \citep{Mead2021}
gives $S_8 = 0.758$ ($0.06\sigma$ DES~Y3). Paper~6 thus brackets the weak-lensing
measurement, $S_8 \in [0.752,\,0.847]$, with the two-sector WDM route landing within
the KiDS-1000 and DES~Y3 uncertainty bands. The open item is a precision question:
a full N-body treatment of baryonic feedback (deferred for computational cost) is
required to pin the linear-theory $S_8$ within this range.

\textbf{(iii) $\phi$DM mass and Lyman-$\alpha$ constraints.}
The algebraic mass $\mphi = 5.60$~eV is in the warm dark matter regime
($\kfs = 0.493\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$).
Current Lyman-$\alpha$ bounds on thermal WDM place constraints at
$m_{\rm WDM} \gtrsim 5.3$~keV \citep{Irsic2017}, but the \SSEE\ $\phi$DM
is a mixed sector (fraction $f_\phi = 0.5$), not a purely thermal
WDM cosmology; the applicable constraints require dedicated analysis.

\textbf{(iv) Quantum field theory completion.}
The algebraic connection $H_0 = 3(\phiG+\pi)^2$ is a successful numerical
retrodiction but lacks a derivation from first-principles quantum field theory.
Paper~10 supplies a UV completion at the EFT level --- the higher-derivative
operator $X^2/M^4$ with algebraic cutoff $M = 8.81$~meV --- but a derivation of

\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}
% ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

We have presented a consolidated validation of the \SSEE-V3.6 framework
across four observational tiers.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=2pt]
\item \textbf{Background sector.} The algebraic prediction
  $(\wz, \wa) = (-0.840, -0.670)$, committed before DESI~DR2, lies within
  $0.05\sigma$ of the DESI~DR2 CPL posterior.
  A Bayesian MCMC with $N_{\rm eff} = 4\,402$ yields
  $H_0 = 66.75\pm0.44$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$ and $\Delta\mathrm{BIC} = -5.55$
  for the dynamic sector.
  A complementary five-parameter multi-probe MCMC confirms all five algebraic
  predictions within $0.81\sigma$ (mean $0.36\sigma$).

\item \textbf{CMB Boltzmann.} CLASS confirms that the MIRA factor
  $\MIRA = 1.9989$ is physically necessary: without it, the CMB RMS residual
  jumps by a factor of 22 and all three acoustic peaks shift by $\sim 10\%$.
  With MIRA, peak positions agree with \LCDM\ to within $\Delta\ell < 5$
  (RMS $= 1.4\%$).

\item \textbf{Structure growth.} The corrected IS perturbation analysis
  ($\OmmCMB=0.320$ in Poisson) gives $\mathcal{G} = 1.011$ and $\sigma_8 = 0.820$,
  with mean $f\sigma_8$ tension $0.74\sigma$ (Paper~5) --- statistically identical
  to $\Lambda$CDM ($0.73\sigma$). The $\phi$DM two-sector extension with
  CLASS-calibrated WDM transfer function ($\alpha = 1.656\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$)
  yields $\sigeff = 0.737$ and reduces $S_8$ from $3.9\sigma$ to $2.6\sigma$
  (KiDS) in the analytic route, with $S_8 = 0.758$ ($0.06\sigma$ DES~Y3)
  after HMcode baryonic feedback (Paper~6).

\item \textbf{EFT stability.} EFTCAMB confirms ghost-freedom and gradient
  stability using the fluid-approximation input $\alphaK^{\rm fluid} = 0.4033$
  ($\Delta = 0.005\%$); the bare-field theoretical value from Paper~7 is
  $\alphaK = 0.073$ (below Euclid sensitivity $\sigma(\alpha_{K,0}) < 0.1$).
  $\alphaT = \alphaM = \alphaB = 0$ (exact); GW170817 compatibility is exact.
  The CPL phantom crossing at $z \approx 0.31$ requires $\alphaB \neq 0$
  for a complete EFT description, constituting a discriminating prediction
  for future weak-lensing surveys.

\item \textbf{Theoretical extensions.} The disformal strong-field regime predicts
  an Einstein-radius amplification $\sqrt{\AURA}\approx\MIRA$, falsifiable with
  HST/JWST strong lensing (Paper~8). An algebraic late-time screening fraction
  $\fscr=(\pi-\phiG)/(\phiG+\pi)^2\approx0.067$ yields
  $H_0^{\rm local}=72.86$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$, $0.17\sigma$ from SH0ES, with
  zero free parameters (Paper~9). A UV completion $K(X)=X/\KAL+X^2/M^4$ with
  algebraic cutoff $M=8.81$~meV connects the dark-energy EFT to the inflationary
  $\alpha$-attractor; the associated $H_0^{\rm UV}=73.040$~km\,s$^{-1}$\,Mpc$^{-1}$
  is a self-consistency check conditional on a separability postulate, not an
  independent prediction (Paper~10).
\end{enumerate}

The framework generates five near-term falsifiable predictions, under the stated
assumptions: the $\phi$-DM free-streaming scale
$\kfs = 0.493\,h\,\mathrm{Mpc}^{-1}$ (DESI~Y3/Euclid $P(k)$; the algebraic mass
$m_\phi = 5.60$~eV enters observation only through $\kfs$, as $\phi$-DM has no
Standard-Model portal), $\alphaK = 0.073$ (Euclid WL null test; a detection $\alphaK > 0.1$ would
falsify SSEE), $r = 0.00813$ (LiteBIRD), and a strong-lensing Einstein-radius
amplification $\sqrt{\AURA}\approx\MIRA$ (HST/JWST).
